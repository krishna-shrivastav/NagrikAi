Festival information at a glance

🏭 GIDA's 37th Foundation Day

30 November 2026
Gorakhpur Industrial Development Authority's 37th Foundation Day celebration.

  • Content Creation Competition among educational institutions
  • Rangoli Competition among educational institutions
  • Focus: creativity, innovation and digital skills
  • BIT, Sector 7, GIDA, Gorakhpur designated as nodal institution
  • Winning team will receive an incentive prize

Coordination: BIT will coordinate with participating institutions and manage competition format, subject matter, participation process and evaluation arrangements.

🎊 Gorakhpur Mahotsav 2027

11–17 January 2027
Venue: Champa Devi Park, Gorakhpur

  • Children’s academic, sports and cultural competitions
  • Competitions and activities for women
  • Local artist showcases and cultural evenings
  • Mega trade fair for businesses, traders, entrepreneurs and craftsmen
  • Bhojpuri and Bollywood cultural nights listed as attractions
